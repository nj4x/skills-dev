Wait — I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that. Start with one short paragraph of context. Then write in the style of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English: sentences of 20 words or fewer, active voice, and one term per concept. Repeat the noun when a pronoun could point to two things. Take the terms from the ubiquitous language in `GLOSSARY.md`. If the repo has more than one glossary, follow `GLOSSARY-MAP.md` to the right one.
